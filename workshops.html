Learn by Making

Copper City Makerspace offers hands-on workshops for people interested in woodworking, metalwork, electronics, coding, and 3D printing. Our workshops help members develop practical skills while learning how to use shared equipment safely.

Workshop Areas

Digital Fabrication

Learn how to use 3D printers, laser cutters, and CNC routers to turn digital designs into physical projects.

Electronics & Coding

Explore microcontrollers, soldering, and projects that combine hardware and software.

Wood & Metal Shops

Practice using saws, drills, and other equipment while learning about shop safety and construction techniques.

Getting Started With a Workshop

New members can follow these steps before using equipment:

  1. Choose a workshop that matches your interests.
  2. Ask about orientation and safety requirements.
  3. Attend the workshop and practice with guidance.
