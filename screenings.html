Watch, Learn, and Get Inspired

Copper City Makerspace brings community members together through screenings focused on creativity, technology, design, and hands-on projects. These events give people an opportunity to learn from others and share their own ideas.

What Our Screenings Offer

Creative Inspiration

Screenings introduce members to new ideas, creative projects, and different ways to solve problems.

Group Discussions

Members can discuss what they watched, ask questions, and share their opinions with other attendees.

Community Learning

Screenings help beginners and experienced makers learn from one another in a welcoming environment.

Turn Inspiration Into Action

After a screening, members can use what they learned as inspiration for a new project or workshop. Sharing ideas can help turn a simple discussion into a hands-on experience.
