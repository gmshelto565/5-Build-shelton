Building Connections Through Creativity

Copper City Makerspace supports community learning by bringing together students, hobbyists, small business owners, and experienced makers. Collaborations help people share knowledge, learn new skills, and work on creative projects.

Ways to Collaborate

Local Schools

Working with schools can give students opportunities to explore technology, design, and hands-on learning.

Experienced Makers

Experienced members can mentor beginners, share techniques, and help lead workshops.

Small Businesses

Local businesses can connect with makers to discuss prototypes, product ideas, and creative problem solving.

Get Involved

Community members interested in teaching, volunteering, or sharing a project idea can contact the makerspace team to discuss possible opportunities.

  • Volunteer to help with a community event.
  • Share a skill or lead a learning activity.
  • Suggest a project with a local organization.
